Creating a startup is about learning.

Creating a business is about trust.

When you combine learning and trust, you reach the moment every startup is looking for: you are ready to go to market.

That is where we are with PredSea.

Over the last weeks, we have been learning fast.

We learned that SOCIB and Copernicus can offer high-resolution, high-quality marine data for the Balearics: waves, currents, buoy observations, and enough context to start building serious route intelligence.

We also learned that the real product is not just the forecast.

Captains do not need another raw map under pressure. They need a trusted operational read:

- Is this crossing comfortable for my vessel?
- What is the best departure window?
- Where is the local risk building?
- What evidence supports the recommendation?

That is why we are building PredSea as a visual and conversational co-captain: marine forecast data, validated against real observations, translated into clear route advice.

This week we checked PredSea route forecasts against SOCIB buoy observations around the Balearics.

A few early numbers:
- 21 matched wave-height checks across Palma-Ibiza, Ibiza-Formentera, and Palma-Cabrera
- Overall mean absolute error: 0.20 m
- Palma-Ibiza: 0.18 m MAE
- Ibiza-Formentera: 0.20 m MAE
- Palma-Cabrera: 0.24 m MAE

The metric matters because trust has to be earned. But the bigger point is the learning loop: forecast, compare with ground truth, improve, and then make the result useful for the people making decisions at sea.

Now it is time to leave the lab and speak with captains.

We are ready to offer value to their work, listen carefully, and understand what PredSea needs to become in real operations.

If you operate yachts in the Balearics, manage routes, or make weather decisions for vessels, I would love to talk.
